# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Accept feedback gracefully, focus criticism on ideas and code, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or publication of others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
